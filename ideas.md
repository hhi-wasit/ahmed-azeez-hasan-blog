# Chosen Direction — Evidence Atlas

## Design Movement

The site follows an **editorial cartography** movement, informed by scientific field atlases, epidemiological reporting, and contemporary long-form research publishing. It presents research as a navigable body of evidence rather than a generic personal portfolio.

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence before ornament:** Data marks, citations, and research categories form the visual language; decoration must clarify hierarchy or place.
2. **Asymmetric exploration:** Editorial columns, ruled margins, and offset content panels replace a conventional centered marketing-page layout.
3. **Calm authority:** Spacious composition, restrained animation, and high-contrast type express rigor without a clinical or impersonal feel.
4. **Accessible legibility:** All content maintains clear contrast, durable reading widths, visible focus states, and simple semantic navigation.

## Color Philosophy

The foundation is a warm archival-paper white rather than cold pure white, which makes research feel considered and approachable. Deep ink blue conveys scholarly confidence; **surveillance teal** is the ownable signal color for interventions, links, and markers; muted clay is reserved for warnings, publication metadata, and directional accents. The balance references maps and public-health reports while retaining warmth.

## Layout Paradigm

The home page is organized as a **research field sheet**. A full-width masthead sits beside an evidence panel; subsequent sections appear as offset strips and annotated cards, connected by a vertical location rule on wide screens. This encourages a reading journey from research focus to work, publications, and contact rather than a stack of generic cards.

## Signature Elements

1. A fine **meridian-line pattern** and sparse dot clusters, appearing in hero and section rules as a reference to surveillance networks.
2. Numbered **evidence markers** for research areas, experience, and selected publications.
3. A **field-note label** treatment: compact uppercase metadata paired with prominent editorial headings.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like consulting a well-prepared research dossier. Navigation anchors scroll smoothly, publication links lift slightly and reveal a directional marker, and mobile navigation remains compact and direct. There are no decorative interactions that interrupt reading.

## Animation

At page load, major content blocks fade in with a short 180–240ms upward transform, staggered by 50ms. Hover and focus transitions use a 160ms custom ease-out. The meridian pattern remains static, and all non-essential motion is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** carries the academic editorial voice in display headings, with optical size and moderate line heights. **Source Sans 3** supports body copy, metadata, and navigation for durable reading on all screens. Headings are large but never all-caps; only metadata labels use tracked uppercase. The hierarchy is: serif display heading, concise sans subhead, readable sans body, and monospaced-style numeric metadata where useful.

## Brand Essence

**Ahmed Azeez Hasan is an epidemiologist and educator translating infectious-disease evidence into stronger community health practice.**

Personality: **rigorous, community-minded, measured**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are specific and evidence-led; CTAs are respectful invitations to read, connect, or collaborate. Microcopy avoids marketing superlatives and makes the subject of each link clear.

Example headline: “Community surveillance begins with trusted local knowledge.”

Example CTA: “Read the selected research”

## Wordmark & Logo

The brand mark is a **concentric surveillance beacon**: three imperfectly nested rings interrupted by a directional notch, suggesting detection, response, and community connection. It appears as a bold teal icon with no text; the wordmark pairs the name in DM Serif Display with a small “PUBLIC HEALTH · EPIDEMIOLOGY” descriptor.
